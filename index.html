Scientific distinction: C-Level, X, V, F and SF labels do not automatically identify or estimate the parameters of the sovereign KAI·ROI equation. Enter researched / validated coefficients explicitly. DIIIP, SIPOC and budgets provide sources of evidence, not substitute measurements.

Enter all formal coefficients and a positive WACC to calculate formal CE for the evaluated element.

ψ = mean(DataActivation, I_net); SPO = CC · ABCD · NPS; KAI* = φ · u · f · ψ · SPO · P · Γ; CE = ((((KAI* · MD) − C) / C) − WACC) / WACC. WACC must use a decimal fraction internally (e.g. 10% → 0.10). These are formal calculations on user-entered inputs, not predictions inferred from the diagram.

Simple allocation: A and Z are absolute values (−100% to +100%). Every Y is a % of the A → Z change; each X, V, F or SF is a % of its immediate parent's allocation. Children should total 100% at every parent. Over 100% is allowed and flagged—not silently capped.Allocation percentages measure responsibility for A → Z; euro amounts distribute the available unit-price envelope. Changing the margin or PVP never changes objective coverage.

02 · Hierarchy & contribution

Click any entry to locate its cartouche.

Every cartouche shows its allocation share. Children should total 100% of their parent. Amber = incomplete budget; coral = overshoot; green = fully allocated. Detached nodes do not contribute to Z.
V13 · Objective hierarchy, colour-coded manual/automatic data flows and financial scenarios: Δ = Z target − A. Every connected Y receives an editable share of Δ; each connected X/V/F/SF receives an editable percentage share of its immediate parent. Each parent has a reference budget of 100%; an internal node's delivered share is the sum of the shares delivered by its direct children. A terminal node delivers its assigned share. Z modelled = A + Δ × total delivered Y shares / 100. Contributions beyond 100% are shown without capping. A negative Δ means planned improvement moves downwards. This is an illustrative planning convention, not an established causal model or a change to the KAI·ROI equations.
03 · DOC ROI / FINANCIAL MODELLING

Unit Budget Studio

Translate the objective hierarchy into a transparent, per-unit resource allocation. This is a planning scenario, independent of KAI·ROI's formal equations.

Click a row's ↗ to locate its source node on the canvas.
Top-down allocation follows connected Y → X → V → F → SF shares without normalising unbalanced groups. A node's planned amount is its share of its parent's planned amount. Bottom-up funding requests are direct, incremental cost claims per unit: a parent's total request includes its own direct claim plus all descendant claims, without double-counting the planned distribution. Missing or excess allocations remain visible; detached elements receive no objective budget. This is a scenario, not an accounting ledger or a KAI·ROI equation.

In Ishikawa 2.0, value begins by turning an A → Z ambition into visible variables, traceable interpretations and measurable next steps. DIIIP guides the transition from an exploratory map towards evidence-based decision-making.
