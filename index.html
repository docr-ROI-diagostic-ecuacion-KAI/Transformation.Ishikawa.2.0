02 · Hierarchy & contribution

Click any entry to locate its cartouche.

Disconnected elements remain independently editable and movable; only connected Y branches affect projected Z. Displayed cartouches show the calculated value. Each element retains its editable input score. Right-side values update immediately after saving.
Transparent draft calculation: Each node with children = 50% of its own input + 50% of the mean of its immediate children's calculated scores. A leaf = its input. Each connected Y contributes its calculated score in percentage points to Z. Modelled Z = clamp(A + ΣY, −100%, +100%); gap = Z modelled − Z target. This first-version rule is a configurable modelling assumption, not a validated causal relationship or a replacement for any KAI·ROI equation.
METHODOLOGY AT THE END OF THE JOURNEY

DIIIP · From qualitative objectives to actionable intelligence

In Ishikawa 2.0, value begins by turning an A → Z ambition into visible variables, traceable interpretations and measurable next steps. DIIIP guides the transition from an exploratory map towards evidence-based decision-making.

D
Data

Origin A, target Z, named Y/X/V/F/SF components, declared ratings and supporting observations.

I
Information

The variables are arranged hierarchically, defined consistently and exported as structured JSON/CSV evidence.

I
Intelligence

Transparent provisional aggregation displays contribution scenarios and the gap between declared aspiration and modelled outcome.

I
Insights

Teams identify priorities, conflicting assumptions, variables requiring measurement and questions for testing.

P
Personalization Actions

Prioritised measurement, ownership and follow-up actions prepare responsible data activation and monetization opportunities.

KAI·ROI EQUATION · CUSTOMER EQUITY

Ishikawa 2.0 prepares objective and variable maps for the KAI·ROI research layer

Ishikawa 2.0 supports the qualitative modelling of business objectives and KAI-related variables, identifying which data and evidence should be collected before any credible monetization, Customer Equity analysis or strategic decision.
